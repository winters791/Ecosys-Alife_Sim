# The Long Winter: a demo of the god powers.
#   ./build/luca_headless --ticks 12000 --script scripts/long_winter.txt --out out/winter
#
# Tuned for a world where cold and dark really hurt: this winter kills most
# of life but not all of it. (Halving the sun and cooling by 15 °C, as this
# script first did, is now a total extinction.)
#
# <tick> terrain|heat <x> <y> <radius> <amount> [duration]
# <tick> sun|climate  <amount> [duration]

2000 terrain 64 64 30 35 2000    # a mountain range slowly rises mid-map
4000 sun -0.3 1500               # the sun fades by a third...
4000 climate -9 1500             # ...and an ice age sets in
7000 heat 100 20 12 10           # a hot spring appears in the south-east
8000 sun 0.3 1500                # the sun returns
8000 climate 9 1500
